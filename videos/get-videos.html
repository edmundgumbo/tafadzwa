Get the videos for the laptop pack

  1. Click a lecture link below. It opens MIT's page.
  2. Find the video and click its Download link (on MIT pages it sits beside or under the video). Pick the smallest size offered.
  3. Save it in this videos folder, renamed to the exact name in the box, for example mit6100-01.mp4.
  4. Open index.html, open that mission: the video now plays inside the watch card with no internet.

STOP: one lecture video is roughly 200 to 500 MB. Download only the next two or three you will watch, on Wi-Fi, and delete ones you finish. MIT videos are CC BY-NC-SA: keep them free and credited, never sell or re-upload them.
